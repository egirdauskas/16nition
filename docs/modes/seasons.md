# Seasons

Single-player league play through 10 divisions. You start in Division 10 and
work up to Division 1.

## How a season works

1. **Pick a competition.** Each division offers 4–6 competitions. Each has 10
   fixtures with fixed difficulties and may have a squad requirement, such
   as chemistry, nationalities or league. Harder fixtures pay more. The full
   list is in the [Seasons reference](../reference/seasons.md#competitions).
2. **Play 10 matches.** Win 3 points, draw 1, loss 0. You can play each match
   yourself or simulate it.
3. **The season ends** after the 10th match, or **early** once your final
   outcome is locked. That happens when the best outcome still in reach is
   already secured, for example the title after 7 straight wins. A season never
   ends early while a better outcome is still possible. Unplayed fixtures stay
   unplayed and do not count as losses.
4. **The outcome** is set by your final points:

| Outcome | Effect |
|---|---|
| Title | Promotion, plus title coins and a title pack |
| Promotion | Move up one division |
| Stay up | Stay in the division |
| Relegation | Move down one division (not possible in Division 10) |

Division 1 has no promotion. Its title is the top prize. Points thresholds per
division are in the [reference](../reference/seasons.md#points-needed-per-division).

## Rewards

| When | Reward | Table |
|---|---|---|
| Every match | Coins and XP by the fixture's difficulty and result. Coins grow 2% per account level above 1. | [Difficulty rewards](../reference/difficulty.md) |
| Every match | One untradeable pack by division and result | [Match packs](../reference/seasons.md#match-and-title-packs-per-division) |
| End of season | Season coins for the outcome: the average over the competition's 10 fixture difficulties | [Per competition](../reference/seasons.md#competitions) |
| Title | One extra untradeable title pack | [Title packs](../reference/seasons.md#match-and-title-packs-per-division) |
| First time reaching a division / winning its title | Accomplishment rewards | [Accomplishments](../reference/accomplishments.md#seasons) |
| Level-ups from match XP | Level coins and milestone packs | [Progression](../reference/progression.md) |

Season coins are paid in addition to the match coins.

## Leaving a season or match

- **Abandon season:** your current points decide the outcome against the
  division's stay-up line. You cannot be promoted this way, and you cannot
  drop below Division 10. No new coins, XP or packs are given. Rewards you
  already earned stay.
- **Quit a match:** it counts as a loss with the score at that moment, and
  gives no XP.
- If the game is closed during a match, the match is rolled back safely when
  you return. Nothing is paid twice.

## Player condition

Fitness and morale stay at 99. Matches do not wear players down, so fitness
and healing items are not needed in Seasons.
