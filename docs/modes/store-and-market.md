# Store, packs and Transfer Market

## Store

Packs can be bought with coins or with FIFA Points (FP). FP are not sold for
money. The only way to get them is [GODMODE](../launcher.md#godmode). Every
pack can contain special cards (TOTW, TOTS and other promos).

| Pack | Items | Quality | Guaranteed rares | Coins |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bronze | 12 | Bronze | 1 | 400 |
| Premium Bronze | 12 | Bronze | 3 | 750 |
| Silver | 12 | Silver | 1 | 2,500 |
| Premium Silver | 12 | Silver | 3 | 3,750 |
| Gold | 12 | Gold | 1 | 5,000 |
| Premium Gold | 12 | Gold | 3 | 7,500 |
| Jumbo Premium Gold | 24 | Gold | 7 | 15,000 |
| Premium Gold Players | 12 | Gold | 3 | 25,000 |
| Mega | 30 | Gold | 18 | 35,000 |
| Rare Players | 12 | Gold | 12 | 50,000 |
| Jumbo Rare Players | 24 | Gold | 24 | 100,000 |
| Ultimate | 30 | Gold | 30 | 125,000 |

FP prices and the current special offers (Starter Pack for 1 coin, once
per club; time-limited Brazil Pack) are in the
[Store reference](../reference/store.md).

Packs earned as rewards are the same packs, but their contents are
**untradeable**: they cannot be listed on the market and quick sell for 0.

Unopened packs wait in **My Packs**.

## Transfer Market

The market is simulated on your device. It is always stocked with about 120
listings from computer sellers, and your own listings sell to computer
buyers.

- Search, bid and buy now work as in the original game.
- Fair prices follow rating, rarity, position, pace and nation. Most sales
  happen early in a listing. A card priced at 70% of fair value or less sells
  within seconds.
- Some computer listings are bargains, and occasionally one is badly
  mispriced. It pays to search.
- A 5% fee is taken from your sales.
- Non-special bronze and silver cards never trade above 10,000 coins.
- Earned (untradeable) items cannot be listed.

## Quick sell

Quick sell gives coins immediately: the card's rating times a factor for its
quality and rarity. Club items (kits, badges, stadiums, balls) can be quick
sold too. The values are in the [market reference](../reference/market.md).
