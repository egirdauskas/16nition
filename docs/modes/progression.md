# Progression

## New club

| Grant | Contents |
|---|---|
| Starting squad | 1 gold player from your favourite club (silver or bronze if it has no gold), 5 silver and 5 bronze starters, 4 silver and 8 bronze subs, in a 4-4-2 |
| Manager | A random bronze manager |
| Club items | Your favourite club's badge, home and away kits and stadium |
| Welcome gift | 7,500 coins and 5 Premium Gold packs, claimed from the welcome message |
| Starter Pack | 6 rare gold players for 1 coin in the Store, once per club |

## XP and levels

Every match gives XP: 100 for a win, 60 for a draw and 30 for a loss, scaled
up by difficulty (up to 187% at Ultimate). Each level needs 50 XP more than the
one before, starting at 300 XP for level 2. There is no level cap.

| Reward | When |
|---|---|
| 1,000 coins × the new level | Every level-up (level 10 pays 10,000) |
| Milestone pack | Levels 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25 |
| Ultimate pack | Every 5 levels from level 30 on |
| Higher match coins | Each level above 1 adds 2% to match coins |

The full table is in the [Progression reference](../reference/progression.md).

## Accomplishments

54 permanent challenges in five groups. Progress counts from the moment the
club is created. When a challenge is complete, collect it with **Redeem**.

| Group | Challenges | Rewards |
|---|---|---|
| Goals | Score 10 – 1,000 goals | 2,500 – 75,000 coins plus packs up to Ultimate |
| Matches | Play, win, keep clean sheets, win by 3+ | 2,000 – 50,000 coins plus packs |
| Seasons | Reach each division; win each division title | 5,000 – 50,000 coins plus packs; every title gives at least a Mega pack |
| Transfer Market | Buy and sell items | 5,000 – 15,000 coins plus Rare Players packs |
| Nation Legends | Hard nation challenges, for example 20 headers with German players | One untradeable special card each, rated 89–92 |

Every challenge is listed in the
[Accomplishments reference](../reference/accomplishments.md).
